How Feehund makes money

Feehund doesn't charge banks, payment companies or any other company. None of them pays to appear or to come out better.

Reading your statement, seeing what you pay and the cancellation guides are free, with no sign-up. And they will stay that way.

Feehund only charges when it does something for you, like preparing the full report on your card machine. You see the price before you start and pay once, with no subscription.

It doesn't sell data either. Your statement is read to give you the result and is not stored.
