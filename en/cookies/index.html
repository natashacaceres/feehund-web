Cookie policy

The website only uses technical storage, needed for it to work, which does not require consent (article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE). That is why there is no cookie banner.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are hosted on the website itself.

How to delete them

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies.
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Safari: Settings › Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions.
