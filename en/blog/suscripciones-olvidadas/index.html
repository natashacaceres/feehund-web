Which subscriptions am I paying for without knowing?

Check your bank statement before January arrives. We tell you where they hide and how to cancel.

At Christmas people try streaming platforms for free, give subscriptions as gifts and sign up for something to watch a match. In January the charges arrive. Before they do, go through your statement with these questions.

Why do I pay for subscriptions I don't use?

Because they renew on their own. A free trial becomes paid on the day it ends if you don't cancel it, and the charge arrives when you have already forgotten.

It happens to more and more of us. In Spain, searches for ‘cancelar suscripción’ (cancel subscription) have almost doubled since 2022, according to Google Trends. January and November are the months with the most searches for ‘darse de baja’ (unsubscribe).

How do I find them on my statement?

Look for charges that repeat every month or every year with the same amount, or almost. Download your transactions for the last 12 months, because annual subscriptions only appear once.

Watch out for three names that group several subscriptions into a single charge.

  1. APPLE.COM/BILL groups iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV+ and App Store apps.
  2. GOOGLE with an asterisk is Google Play and Google services.
  3. PAYPAL with an asterisk can hide any merchant.

To see what is inside, check the subscriptions in your Apple or Google account, or your PayPal history.

What do I do with Christmas free trials?

Note the day each one ends. With Apple you have to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. With Spotify, if you cancel during the trial, you move to the free version straight away.

If you signed up through Apple, Google or your mobile operator, you cancel it there and not in the service's app.

How do I cancel?

It depends on the company. At Feehund we have checked 184 companies one by one. 75 can only be cancelled from your account or from the app store. Only 31 accept cancellation by email. The rest ask for a phone call, a paper letter or their own form.

You have it company by company in Cancel, with the official source and the date it was checked.

If I block my card or reverse the direct debit, have I cancelled?

No. The contract continues and the company can claim what you owe. Cancel first and keep the proof.

If it is a direct debit, you have eight weeks from the charge to ask the bank to refund it, under article 49 of Real Decreto-ley 19/2018 (Royal Decree-law). We explain it with insurance in My insurance has gone up. Can I reverse the direct debit?

What if they keep charging me after I cancel?

Complain to the company with the proof of cancellation. If they keep charging you for a service you asked to cancel, the customer service law still treats you as a customer so you can complain (Ley 10/2025, article 3).

If it is a charge you did not authorise, tell your bank as soon as possible. The maximum time limit is thirteen months from the charge, under article 43 of Real Decreto-ley 19/2018.

What should I check first in January?

  1. New charges since November, especially free trials that are now charging.
  2. What has gone up in price since last year.
  3. Two services for the same thing, for example two series platforms.
