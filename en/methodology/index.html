How it is calculated

The calculator compares what a business pays to take card payments with the public pricing of the banks and companies that, by an in-house estimate, process more than 75 % of the amount paid by card on card machines in Spain.

Only pricing published on each company's official website is included, checked every month. No bank or payment company pays to appear or for its position.

The market breakdown, the sources and the limits of the calculation are in the methodology note.

Download the methodology note (PDF, 1 page, in Spanish)

How it works

  1. Choose or type your statement

    Copy from your monthly statement what you took by card, the number of payments and what your bank charged.

  2. See how much extra you pay (free)

    The calculator compares your case with the public pricing of the banks and companies that process more than 75 % of card payments in Spain and tells you how much extra you pay each month.

  3. If it's worth it, find out who to switch to (4 €, paid by you, not a bank)

    The report gives you the exact bank and plan, when to leave and what to ask for when negotiating.

Common questions

Another question? Write to Feehund and you will get a reply within 7 days.

How much is normal to pay for a card machine?

It depends on how much you sell, how much each sale is and which cards your customers use. The calculator compares your case with the public pricing of the banks and card machine companies that process more than 75 % of card payments in Spain. If your bank gave you a special price, you can enter your own. How it is calculated →

What do I need?

Your card machine's monthly statement, which you'll find in your bank's app or website. And your contract, if you want to know what leaving would cost.

Does this site make money if I switch banks?

No. No bank or provider pays . The only charge is 4 € for the recommendation, and you pay it. Feehund's owner is a founder of Ipso, a card payments company for hospitality. Ipso is not among the tariffs compared. More on this in La Letra Pequeña →

I'm tied into a contract. Is it worth leaving?

Enter the exit fee and the months you have left. The result will tell you whether to leave now or wait until it ends.

How much does it cost?

Finding out if you're paying too much is free. If switching is worth it, you can see who to switch to for 4 €, VAT included.

Can anyone see what I type?

No. The calculation happens on your phone or computer. Your numbers aren't sent anywhere and you don't need to sign up.
