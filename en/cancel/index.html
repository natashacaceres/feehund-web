Frequently asked questions

What do I get for 4,99 €?

Your cancellation letter sent by certified mail to the company, and the PDF certificate in your inbox. If it does not arrive, we refund you. If they charge you again, the second letter is free.

Is it legal for you to send it?

The letter is yours and you sign it with the code. Feehund only sends it by certified mail.

What if they keep charging me?

Choose your next statement from the link in the email. If there is a charge after the cancellation date, we send you a second letter for free, quoting the certificate. You can also return the direct debit from your bank.

Can cancelling cost me more?

Yes, if you have a minimum term: the company may charge a penalty if you leave before it ends. Before sending, we warn you and suggest a date. If the company asks for days' notice, we send it early enough to arrive in time.
