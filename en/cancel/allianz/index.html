How to cancel Allianz

Our sending service does not work with Allianz because it asks for the cancellation by paper letter. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check the expiry date on your policy.

  2. 2

    Have your policy number and your DNI (Spanish ID card) to hand, as the policyholder.

  3. 3

    Call 900 300 250 at least one month before the expiry date and ask to cancel.

  4. 4

    If you prefer to do it in writing, send a signed letter with your details, DNI and policy number, by a means with acknowledgement of receipt.

What you need to know

Phone
900 300 250. Monday to Thursday from 9 to 19 h and Fridays from 9 to 18 h. It is the channel given on their cancellation page.
Letter
Send it to Allianz, Compañía de Seguros y Reaseguros SA, C/ Ramírez de Arellano 35, 28043 Madrid, or to a branch.
Your intermediary
According to the car insurance conditions, notices can also be given through the distributor of your policy.
How much notice to give
At least 1 month before the policy expiry date.
Minimum term
For car insurance, the policy expires every year and extends automatically unless you object one month in advance.
Your proof
If it is by letter, the acknowledgement of receipt.
What you need
Being the policyholder. Policy number. DNI.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: allianz.es

Keep in mind

  • Not paying the premium is not the same as cancelling.
