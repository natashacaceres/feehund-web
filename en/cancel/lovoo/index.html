How to cancel Lovoo

Our sending service does not work with Lovoo because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check which provider you paid with. The contract was made with them and you cancel with them.

  2. 2

    Adyen. In the app, tap the LOVOO Premium button under your photo, scroll down to Manage subscription and tap Cancel subscription.

  3. 3

    Stripe. On the Lovoo website, gear at the top right, Premium, Cancel subscription, and follow Stripe's instructions.

  4. 4

    PayPal. Go to paypal.com, gear at the top right, and cancel the automatic payments to Lovoo.

  5. 5

    Do it up to 24 hours before the end of the period.

What you need to know

Depending on how you paid
Google Play in Google Play. PayPal in your PayPal account. Card with Adyen in the app. Card with Stripe only on the Lovoo website.
How much notice to give
Up to 24 hours before the end of the subscription period.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. The Premium benefits continue until the end of the paid period. After that your profile keeps the basic features.
What you need
Access to the payment provider or to your Lovoo account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.lovoo.com

Keep in mind

  • Deleting your Lovoo profile does not cancel the subscription.
  • If you paid with Adyen or Stripe, you can also send your last purchase receipt to the customer service team asking for the cancellation.
