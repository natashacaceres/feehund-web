How to cancel Holmes Place

Our sending service does not work with Holmes Place because the cancellation goes to your club and we do not have every club's address yet. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check your contract for the notice period and whether your fee has special conditions.

  2. 2

    Ask your club to cancel in writing, with your name, DNI (Spanish ID card) and membership number.

  3. 3

    Ask for a receipt or written confirmation.

What you need to know

Your club
Ask to cancel in writing at reception or by email to your club.
Minimum term
It depends on the fee.
Your proof
The club's written confirmation.
What you need
Name and DNI. Membership number.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: boe.es

Keep in mind

  • By law you can cancel through the same channel you used to sign up.
