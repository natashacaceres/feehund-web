How to cancel Prime Video

Our sending service does not work with Prime Video because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to amazon.es with your account and open «Suscripción a Amazon Prime».

  2. 2

    Tap «Gestionar suscripción» and then «Actualiza, cancela y más».

  3. 3

    Follow the screens until you confirm. For channels, check the end date in the confirmation.

What you need to know

Amazon.es (if you have Amazon Prime)
Prime Video is included in Amazon Prime. To remove it, cancel Prime at amazon.es > «Suscripción a Amazon Prime > Gestionar suscripción > Actualiza, cancela y más» (Amazon Prime membership > Manage membership > Update, cancel and more).
Prime Video (Prime Video only)
If you have a Prime Video only subscription, «Cuenta y configuración > Mi cuenta > Finalizar suscripción» (Account and settings > My account > End subscription).
Prime Video channels
Extra channels (for example HBO Max or Atresplayer inside Prime Video) are cancelled in «Cuenta y configuración > Mis suscripciones > Anular suscripción» (Account and settings > My subscriptions > Cancel subscription).
Apple or provider
If you pay through Apple or your mobile provider, cancel with them. With Apple, at least 24 hours before renewal.
How much notice to give
Cancel before renewal. Channels stay active until the end date they show you and you can reverse it until then.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. Prime renews automatically, monthly or yearly. If you haven't used any Prime benefit, you are refunded the whole period within 5 to 7 working days. If you signed up less than 14 days ago you can withdraw; if you used benefits, you get back less.
Equipment
There is no equipment to return.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of the cancellation confirmation.
What you need
Log in to your Amazon account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: amazon.es

Keep in mind

  • Cancelling a channel does not refund what has already been charged.
  • If you cancel Prime, the channels linked to Prime stop renewing when Prime ends.
  • You can ask for an email reminder 3 days before renewal with «Recordar más tarde» (Remind me later).
