How to cancel Zoom

  1. 1

    Go to the Zoom website and open your account's billing portal.

  2. 2

    Cancel the plan before renewal.

  3. 3

    If you cannot, write to renewals@zoom.us asking to cancel the renewal.

  4. 4

    Do it at least 30 calendar days before the next period.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Zoom billing portal
On the Zoom website, from your account's billing portal, if it is available to you.
Email
renewals@zoom.us. The terms say you can give written notice to cancel the renewal.
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
30 calendar days before the start of the next renewal period, or the period set by law.
Minimum term
Payments cannot be cancelled during the current period and are not refunded, unless Zoom agrees or the law requires it.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep the email sent to renewals@zoom.us and their reply, or a screenshot of the portal.
What you need
Your Zoom account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: zoom.com

Keep in mind

  • If you give less than 30 days' notice, it renews for another period.
