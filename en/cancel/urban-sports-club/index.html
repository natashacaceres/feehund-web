How to cancel Urban Sports Club

  1. 1

    Log in to urbansportsclub.com with your account.

  2. 2

    Tap your photo at the top right and go to «Your Account».

  3. 3

    Scroll to the bottom and cancel the membership.

  4. 4

    Do it at least 3 days before your monthly cycle ends.

  5. 5

    Keep the confirmation email.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Web
Log in to your account from the website (not from the app). At the top right tap your photo, then «Your Account». The option to cancel is at the bottom of the page.
Email
The terms say the cancellation can be given in writing and that an email is enough. The address they give is member-contact@urbansportsclub.com.
How much notice to give
For the monthly membership, 3 days before the billing cycle ends.
Minimum term
The 1 and 2 year memberships can't be cancelled monthly. When the term ends they switch to monthly automatically, unless you cancel or extend them.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep the confirmation email or a copy of the email you sent.
What you need
Your Urban Sports Club account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: urbansportsclub.com

Keep in mind

  • You can't cancel from the app, you have to use the website.
  • If you cancel during a pause, the cancellation takes effect at the end of the next billing period after the pause.
  • Your balance of vouchers and referrals is lost when you cancel.
