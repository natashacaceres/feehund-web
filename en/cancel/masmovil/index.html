How to cancel MásMóvil

Our sending service does not work with MásMóvil because only accepts landline cancellations by email; mobile cancellations go by paper letter. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Call customer service and ask to cancel, or send the cancellation template with a copy of your DNI.

  2. 2

    If it is only the landline, fill in the form on the website and send it to solicitudbaja@masmovil.com.

  3. 3

    Return the router at Correos (Spanish post office) with the code you get by text message.

  4. 4

    Download your latest bills first: you will not be able to afterwards.

What you need to know

Phone
Customer service: 2373 from a MásMóvil line or 911 333 333 from another provider.
Form by email
Landline only: the form on the website, filled in, to solicitudbaja@masmovil.com.
In writing
The contract allows you to send the cancellation notice (template on the website) with a copy of your DNI (Spanish ID card) to «Atención al Cliente» (Customer Service).
How much notice to give
At least 2 working days. You are charged the proportional part of the days of the last month.
Minimum term
If you have a minimum term commitment, you get another bill with the remaining penalty. On 1Gb fibre, if you leave before 3 months, 242 € for installation (figure from the website, see sources).
Equipment
Router: free return at Correos with the code from the text message. If it is not returned, 168,19 €. The contract gives 1 month from the cancellation.
Your proof
Reference number or a copy of the email you sent.
What you need
Being the account holder. Copy of your DNI if it is in writing.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: masmovil.es

Keep in mind

  • If you cancel the line you lose the number: to keep it, port it to another provider.
  • MásMóvil TV has its own cancellation process.
