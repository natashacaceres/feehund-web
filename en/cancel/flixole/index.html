How to cancel FlixOlé

Our sending service does not work with FlixOlé because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to flixole.com with your details and open «Mi cuenta», top right.

  2. 2

    Click «Mi plan» and then «Cancelar el plan».

  3. 3

    Confirm with «Cancelar suscripción». You will see «(Cancelado) Disponible hasta» (Cancelled, available until) and the date your period ends.

What you need to know

FlixOlé website
flixole.com, «Mi cuenta» (My account, top right), «Mi plan», «Cancelar el plan», «Cancelar suscripción» (My plan, Cancel the plan, Cancel subscription).
App Store or Google Play
If you signed up through Apple or Google Play, you cancel in that store.
How much notice to give
Cancel the day before renewal at the latest. You can keep watching FlixOlé until the end of the monthly or yearly billing period.
Minimum term
No minimum term is stated. The subscription is monthly or yearly and renews automatically. They don't refund part months or part years.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of «Mi plan» with the message «(Cancelado) Disponible hasta».
What you need
Log in to your FlixOlé account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: flixole.com

Keep in mind

  • If a renewal charge is in progress or has failed, the cancel button won't appear until it is charged.
  • There is no right of withdrawal for this service, according to their terms.
