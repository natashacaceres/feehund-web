How to cancel Dreamfit

Our sending service does not work with Dreamfit because it only handles cancellations in person. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Decide the last month you want to pay.

  2. 2

    Ask to cancel by day 20 of the month before at the latest, at reception or in your personal area.

  3. 3

    Keep the receipt or the confirmation.

What you need to know

Your gym's reception
Fill in the cancellation form.
Personal area on the website
From your account at dreamfit.es.
How much notice to give
By day 20 of the month before the month you want to finish at the latest.
Minimum term
Renewal is automatic for your fee's payment period. If you give notice late, you pay the following month.
Equipment
If you rent a locker, cancelling it is separate and is signed at reception.
Your proof
The reception receipt or the confirmation from the personal area.
What you need
Membership details. Access to the personal area or going to the gym.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: dreamfit.es

Keep in mind

  • Dreamfit does not accept cancellations through any channel other than reception or the personal area.
  • Locker rental is not refunded.
