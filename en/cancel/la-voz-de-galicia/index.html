How to cancel La Voz de Galicia

  1. 1

    Log in to your lavozdegalicia.es account and cancel the subscription before it expires.

  2. 2

    If you cannot, write to suscripciones@lavoz.es or call 900 154 218.

  3. 3

    Through those other channels, give at least 5 days' notice before expiry.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Website
Your account on lavozdegalicia.es, before the period expires.
Phone
900 154 218, subscriber service.
Email
suscripciones@lavoz.es.
Letter
La Voz de Galicia SA, Avenida de la Prensa 84-85, Polígono Industrial de Sabón, 15143 Arteixo (A Coruña).
Apple or Google
If you paid for it in the app, you cancel it with Apple or Google Play.
How much notice to give
From your account, before expiry. By phone, email or letter, at least 5 days before.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term, but each period renews automatically for the same length of time.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep the screenshot of your account or the copy of your email.
What you need
Your username or your subscription details.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: lavozdegalicia.es

Keep in mind

  • If you joined with an offer, it renews at the normal price without discount.
  • The period already paid is not refunded. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the period.
  • With the digital subscription there is no right of withdrawal once you start reading.
