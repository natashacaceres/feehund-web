How to cancel Mistral Le Chat

Our sending service does not work with Mistral Le Chat because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Cancel where you subscribed.

  2. 2

    On the web, open «Subscriptions» in the «Admin panel» and the «Subscription» tab.

  3. 3

    Under «Downgrade to Free», select «Unsubscribe» and confirm.

  4. 4

    You will get a confirmation email.

What you need to know

Mistral website («Admin panel»)
«Subscriptions» in the «Admin panel», «Subscription» tab, «Downgrade to Free» section, «Unsubscribe».
iPhone or Android app
Menu at the top left, your organisation's name, «Subscription», «Manage subscription». It takes you to the App Store or Google Play to cancel.
Mobile operator
If the Pro plan comes with your mobile tariff or your operator bills you for it, you cancel it with the operator.
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
After 14 days from purchase, you keep the plan until the end of the billing period and then it switches to free.
Minimum term
None.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The cancellation confirmation email.
What you need
Your Mistral account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: help.mistral.ai

Keep in mind

  • Le Chat is now called Vibe. Your account and your plan stay the same.
  • If you cancel on the web within 14 days of your first purchase, the cancellation is immediate and they refund you automatically. This does not apply to renewals.
  • If you paid through the App Store or Google Play, the store handles the refund.
  • If your account is managed by your company or school, talk to its administrator.
