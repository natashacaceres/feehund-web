How to cancel Bitdefender

Our sending service does not work with Bitdefender because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to central.bitdefender.com with your email and password.

  2. 2

    In the left-hand menu click «My Subscriptions» and open the «My Payments» tab. If you see «Billing», use the «Subscriptions» tab.

  3. 3

    Click «View details» next to the product.

  4. 4

    Click «Manage your subscription renewal options» and then «End Auto-Renewal».

  5. 5

    Choose a reason and tap «End Auto-Renewal» twice. You will get a confirmation email.

What you need to know

Bitdefender Central (web)
central.bitdefender.com, «My Subscriptions», «My Payments» tab, «View details», «Manage your subscription renewal options», «End Auto-Renewal».
Bitdefender store (2Checkout/Verifone)
If you cannot do it from Central: store.bitdefender.com/myaccount, ask for the access link and in «My Products» tap «Stop automatic subscription renewal».
Reminder email
45 days before expiry they send you an email with a link to cancel the renewal.
How much notice to give
Before expiry. They remind you by email 45 days before.
Minimum term
None. If you cancel the renewal, you will have to renew manually.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The confirmation email.
What you need
Your Bitdefender Central account or the email used for the purchase.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: bitdefender.com

Keep in mind

  • In the «Subscriptions» tab you can choose to cancel immediately if fewer than 30 days have passed since purchase. Then they refund you and the subscription expires.
  • If you were charged for a renewal you did not want, see Bitdefender's refunds help.
  • If the purchase email is not the one on your account, they ask for a code sent to the purchase email.
