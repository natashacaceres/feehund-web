How to cancel Wellhub

Our sending service does not work with Wellhub because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to the Wellhub platform with your account.

  2. 2

    Go to settings and follow the instructions to cancel.

  3. 3

    Do it at least 3 working days before your billing date.

What you need to know

Wellhub website or app
Log in and follow the cancellation instructions in your account settings.
How much notice to give
3 working days before the billing date. On the free trial, 48 hours before it ends.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep a screenshot or the notice saying the subscription is cancelled.
What you need
Your Wellhub account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: wellhub.com

Keep in mind

  • Your plan stays active until the next billing date, but they don't refund the unused part.
  • If your employer pays for the membership, it may have its own cancellation conditions.
  • If you cancel by mistake, tell them straight away, but they don't guarantee getting it back.
