How to cancel Raiola

Our sending service does not work with Raiola because it is a service for businesses and sending for businesses will come later. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Download a backup of your website, your databases and your email.

  2. 2

    Log in to the customer area and cancel the service.

  3. 3

    Do it before the invoice is issued, 15 days before expiry.

  4. 4

    If you are within the 30-day guarantee, open a ticket to ask for the refund.

What you need to know

Customer area
Cancel the service from your Raiola Networks customer area.
Ticket
If you do not know how to cancel it, open a ticket from the customer area and they will explain.
How much notice to give
15 days before the service expires, before the invoice is issued.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term on any service. It is contracted month by month.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep a screenshot of the cancellation or the ticket.
What you need
Your customer area account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: raiolanetworks.com

Keep in mind

  • The 30-day guarantee is only for new sign-ups, not for renewals.
  • If you ask for a refund, they delete your data at once, including from the backups.
