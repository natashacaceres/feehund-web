How to cancel Nintendo Switch Online

Our sending service does not work with Nintendo Switch Online because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Sign in with the Nintendo Account that bought the subscription.

  2. 2

    Website. On accounts.nintendo.com/portal choose Nintendo Switch Online and the subscription under «Suscripciones activas».

  3. 3

    Tap «Cancelar la renovación automática» and confirm.

  4. 4

    Console. Open Nintendo eShop, choose that account, tap your icon at the top right and select «Cancelar renovación automática».

What you need to know

Console
Nintendo eShop, your user icon at the top right, «Cancelar renovación automática» (Cancel automatic renewal).
Website
accounts.nintendo.com/portal, Nintendo Switch Online, «Suscripciones activas» (Active subscriptions), «Cancelar la renovación automática» (Cancel automatic renewal).
Minimum term
When you cancel automatic renewal, the subscription ends at the end of the current period.
What you need
The Nintendo Account that bought the subscription.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: nintendo.com

Keep in mind

  • Only the holder of the account that bought the subscription can cancel. The other members of the family group cannot.
  • If you cancel automatic renewal, you will not be able to turn it back on.
