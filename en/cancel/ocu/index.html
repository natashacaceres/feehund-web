How to cancel OCU (cuota de socio)

Our sending service does not work with OCU (cuota de socio) because it asks for the cancellation by paper letter. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Call 91 300 91 51 or use the contact form and choose «baja de mi suscripción».

  2. 2

    Do it at least 15 working days before your period ends.

  3. 3

    Keep the receipt from the form, the letter or the day of the call.

What you need to know

Phone
91 300 91 51, subscriptions service. The help pages give Monday to Thursday from 9 to 16 h and Friday from 9 to 14 h.
Web form
Member contact form at ocu.org/contacto/contacto-socio, option «baja de mi suscripción» (cancel my subscription).
Letter
To OCU, Departamento de Suscripciones (Subscriptions Department), C/ Albarracín 21, 28037 Madrid.
How much notice to give
15 working days before the current period ends, to avoid the next charge.
Minimum term
None. The subscription renews automatically every month or every year.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
A copy of the form you sent, of the letter or a note of the call.
What you need
Your member details.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: ocu.org

Keep in mind

  • After cancelling you remain an «Amigo de OCU» (Friend of OCU, free), unless you give that up as well.
  • Within 14 calendar days of signing up you can withdraw without giving a reason.
  • The terms give different phone hours (Monday to Thursday from 9 to 18 h).
