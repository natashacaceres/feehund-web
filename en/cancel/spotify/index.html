How to cancel Spotify

Our sending service does not work with Spotify because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to spotify.com with your account from a browser.

  2. 2

    Go to «Administrar tu plan» and tap «Cancelar suscripción».

  3. 3

    Confirm. Your account switches to the free plan on the next billing date.

What you need to know

Spotify website
spotify.com > «Cuenta > Administrar tu plan > Cancelar suscripción» (Account > Manage your plan > Cancel subscription).
Spotify form
Their help pages also offer a form to ask to cancel Premium and send it to Spotify (you have to log in with your account to open it).
Provider or another company
If you don't see the option to change plan, Premium comes with a partner company (your mobile or internet provider). Your account page shows its contact link; cancel with them.
How much notice to give
You keep Premium until the next billing date. If you cancel during a free trial, you switch to free straight away.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. It renews automatically. They don't refund part periods. You have 14 days from signing up to withdraw: write to angerblankett@spotify.com or by letter, but if you have already used it you pay for the part used.
Equipment
There is no equipment to return.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of «Administrar tu plan» showing the end date.
What you need
Log in to your Spotify account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.spotify.com

Keep in mind

  • If you are a member of a «Familiar» (Family) or «Duo» plan, cancelling only removes you. Only the person who manages it can cancel the plan.
  • If you cancel the «Familiar» plan, all members switch to free.
  • You don't lose your playlists or your saved music.
  • If you are being charged and your account shows as Free, you have Premium on another Spotify account.
