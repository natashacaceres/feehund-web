How to cancel Hits Mobile

  1. 1

    Tell Hits Mobile you want to cancel, by phone, by email or by letter to Customer Service.

  2. 2

    Do it at least 2 working days before the day you want it to take effect.

  3. 3

    Ask for the reference number or keep your email.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Phone
Customer Service 900 696 068, according to the contract. The website also gives 900 670 000, Monday to Sunday from 9 to 20 h.
Email
clientes@hitsmobile.es.
Letter
Send it to Xfera Móviles, S.A., Atención al Cliente (customer service), Parque empresarial La Finca, Paseo del Club Deportivo 1, Edif. 8, 28233 Pozuelo de Alarcón (Madrid).
How much notice to give
At least 2 working days before the day you want it to take effect.
Minimum term
It depends on your sign-up contract. If you had a discount on the fibre installation and you cancel within the following 12 months, you pay that discount back. If you bought a phone or a modem at a discount or in instalments, you pay back the proportional part of the discount and the outstanding instalments fall due.
Your proof
Copy of the email or reference number.
What you need
Being the account holder.
