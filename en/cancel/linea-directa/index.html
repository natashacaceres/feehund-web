How to cancel Línea Directa

Our sending service does not work with Línea Directa because it asks for the cancellation by paper letter. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check the expiry date on your policy.

  2. 2

    Ask to cancel at least 30 days before, preferably in writing. Include your details, the policy number and that you do not want to renew.

  3. 3

    Send it by letter to Tres Cantos or from the «Área de Cliente» (customer area).

  4. 4

    Keep the acknowledgement or the reference number and check in the «Área de Cliente» that the renewal does not appear.

What you need to know

Letter
Línea Directa Aseguradora SA, Ronda de Europa 7, 28760 Tres Cantos (Madrid).
Customer area
On the Línea Directa website or app.
Phone
919 180 991.
How much notice to give
At least 1 month (30 days) before the expiry date. If you give notice late, the policy renews and you have to pay the premium.
Minimum term
Policies are annual. Outside the 14 days for withdrawal, cancelling before the expiry date does not give you the right to a premium refund, except in cases such as selling the vehicle or a total loss.
Your proof
Copy of the letter with acknowledgement of receipt, or the reference number of the request.
What you need
Policyholder's details. Policy number. An express request not to renew.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: lineadirecta.com

Keep in mind

  • Not paying the premium is not the same as cancelling.
  • By phone is valid, but afterwards it is hard to prove the date.
