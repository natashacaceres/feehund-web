How to cancel Ocaso

  1. 1

    Check the expiry date on your policy.

  2. 2

    Write a cancellation request with your name, DNI, policy number and signature. Attach a copy of your DNI.

  3. 3

    Send it to ocaso@ocaso.es at least one month before the expiry date.

  4. 4

    Keep the email you sent and the reply.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.
