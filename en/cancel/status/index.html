How to return the direct debit from your bank
  1. Open your bank's app or website and find the direct debit.
  2. Tap «Return direct debit». If it is not there, ask at your branch.
  3. For direct debits you have 8 weeks from the charge.
  4. Keep the cancellation certificate in case the company disputes it.
