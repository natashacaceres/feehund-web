How to cancel O2

  1. 1

    Write to bajas@o2online.es (or by WhatsApp or on 1551) saying whether it is the landline or the mobile line and attach a photo of the account holder's DNI (Spanish ID card).

  2. 2

    They process the cancellation once they check the documents, without trying to keep you.

  3. 3

    Return the router at a Movistar shop within 15 days starting 24 hours after the cancellation (better with an appointment).

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Email
bajas@o2online.es, with the line number and an image of the account holder's identity document.
WhatsApp
The WhatsApp link on their help page, with the same details.
Phone
1551, free, with the account holder's and the line's details.
How much notice to give
2 working days. You get a last bill for the days of the cycle when you had service.
Minimum term
O2 has no minimum term on any plan.
Equipment
Router at a Movistar shop within 15 days. If not: HGU router 97,30 €, fibre router 78,65 €, ONT 97,28 €. If you move to Movistar you do not have to return it.
Your proof
Keep the email or the reference number from the call.
What you need
Being the account holder. Image of your identity document.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: o2online.es

Keep in mind

  • If you cancel the mobile line you lose the number; to keep it ask your new provider for number portability.
  • Fibre and mobile are cancelled separately if you only want to drop one.
