How to cancel Forus

Our sending service does not work with Forus because it only handles cancellations in person. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Go to reception between day 1 and day 25 of the month.

  2. 2

    Show your ID and sign the cancellation form.

  3. 3

    Be up to date with your payments. If not, they won't process the cancellation.

  4. 4

    Keep your copy of the form.

What you need to know

Centre reception
In person, signing the cancellation form there and then.
How much notice to give
From day 1 to day 25 of the month. The cancellation takes effect when the current fee ends.
Minimum term
When you cancel you lose the joining fee.
Your proof
Your copy of the signed cancellation form.
What you need
DNI (Spanish ID card). Be up to date with payments. Sign the form at reception.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: forus.es

Keep in mind

  • With family memberships, the person who pays must ask to cancel.
  • If you have a serious illness or injury with a medical certificate, you can stop paying the fee for up to 9 months without cancelling.
