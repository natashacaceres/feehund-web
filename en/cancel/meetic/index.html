How to cancel Meetic

Our sending service does not work with Meetic because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    From a computer, go to «Ajustes de mi cuenta» and then to «Gestionar mi suscripción».

  2. 2

    Follow the process and tap «Continuar» (Continue) or «Confirmar» (Confirm) when asked.

  3. 3

    They confirm the cancellation by email.

What you need to know

Website
«Ajustes de mi cuenta» (My account settings), «Gestionar mi suscripción» (Manage my subscription).
App
«Yo» (Me), settings icon, «Gestión de suscripción» (Subscription management).
Google Play or iPhone
If you paid in the store, you cancel it in the store.
How much notice to give
You can cancel from 24 hours after the purchase and no later than 48 hours before renewal.
Minimum term
No. You keep using the subscription until its expiry date. If you chose to pay in instalments, there may be payments outstanding. You have a 14-day cooling-off period after the purchase, with a proportional refund on first subscriptions and upgrades, not on renewals.
Your proof
The email in which Meetic confirms the cancellation.
What you need
Access to your account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: meetic.es

Keep in mind

  • During the 14-day cooling-off period, if you bought on the website, you use the «Formulario de Desistimiento» (Withdrawal Form) in your account settings.
  • If you paid through the App Store or Google Play, the cancellation and refund are handled by the store.
