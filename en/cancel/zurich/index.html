How to cancel Zurich

Our sending service does not work with Zurich because it asks for the cancellation through its own form. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check the expiry date on your policy.

  2. 2

    Send the non-renewal request through Zurich's «Renovación o cancelación» form or to your agent, at least one month before.

  3. 3

    Include your name, DNI (Spanish ID card) and policy number.

  4. 4

    Keep the confirmation.

What you need to know

Web form
«Renovación o cancelación» (Renewal or cancellation) on zurich.es/telefonos-contacto.
Your Zurich agent
Zurich has more than 1.000 agents in Spain.
How much notice to give
By law, at least 1 month before the expiry date.
Minimum term
Annual policies that renew automatically.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of the form you sent and keep the reply.
What you need
Policyholder's details and DNI. Policy number.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: zurich.es

Keep in mind

  • If your life insurance is linked to a mortgage, check whether cancelling it raises your interest rate.
