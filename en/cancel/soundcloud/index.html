How to cancel SoundCloud

Our sending service does not work with SoundCloud because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Go to soundcloud.com from a browser and open your «Suscripciones» page.

  2. 2

    Tap «Edit» next to your subscription.

  3. 3

    Choose «Opt-out of your current plan» at the bottom of the page, then «Confirm» and «Cancel your Plan».

  4. 4

    Reload the page. If it says «EXPIRES» next to the billing date, it is cancelled.

What you need to know

Browser (web)
soundcloud.com on your computer or in your phone's browser, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions) page. You can't cancel from the app.
App Store or Google Play
If you paid through Apple or Google Play, cancel directly with them. SoundCloud can't cancel or refund purchases made on iOS.
How much notice to give
Cancel before the next billing date. You keep the paid features until the period you have already paid for ends.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. It renews automatically for equal periods. They don't refund subscriptions already provided. You have 14 days from purchase to withdraw and they refund everything you paid.
Your proof
A screenshot of the «Suscripciones» page with «EXPIRES» next to the date.
What you need
Log in to your SoundCloud account from a browser.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: pages.soundcloud.com

Keep in mind

  • If you distributed or monetised music with SoundCloud for Artists, it is removed from other platforms 30 days after the subscription ends.
  • If you had Go through the website or Android and switch to Go+ with Apple, the Go subscription ends straight away and what is left is not refunded.
