How to cancel Endesa X (mantenimientos y servicios)

Our sending service does not work with Endesa X (mantenimientos y servicios) because it only handles cancellations by phone. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Call 900 90 90 57 and ask to cancel the service or the maintenance.

  2. 2

    If you have not used the service in the current contract year, the cancellation is immediate.

  3. 3

    If you have used it, the cancellation is scheduled for the day the contract expires. Until then you keep the service.

What you need to know

Phone
900 90 90 57, free of charge. Only the contract holder.
How much notice to give
The contract is annual and renews automatically every year. To stop it renewing you must give 1 month's notice before the expiry date.
Minimum term
Annual contract, even if you pay in instalments. If you pay every month or every two months and they have already done the service or the yearly visit, cancelling early means you pay the remaining instalments until the end of the year.
What you need
Being the contract holder.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: endesa.com

Keep in mind

  • Within 14 calendar days of signing up you can withdraw at no cost.
