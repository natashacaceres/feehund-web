How to cancel Squarespace

Our sending service does not work with Squarespace because it is a service for businesses and sending for businesses will come later. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Open the «Facturación» panel of the site you want to cancel.

  2. 2

    In «Suscripciones», click the plan.

  3. 3

    Click «Cancelar suscripción» and then «Continuar» (Continue).

  4. 4

    Choose a reason and click «Siguiente» (Next).

  5. 5

    Review your other subscriptions and confirm with «Cancelar suscripción».

What you need to know

Site «Facturación» (Billing) panel
«Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the plan, «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription).
How much notice to give
Cancel before the renewal date so you are not charged. The service continues until the end of the billing cycle.
Minimum term
None. The annual plan is paid in full and is only refunded if you cancel within 14 days of purchase.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Keep a screenshot of the cancellation and your invoices.
What you need
Access to the site's «Facturación» panel.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.squarespace.com

Keep in mind

  • The domain, Google Workspace and other subscriptions stay active and keep being charged. You have to cancel them separately.
  • Monthly plans and renewals are not refunded.
  • If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days, the free domain is deducted from the refund.
  • The Mobile Start plan is paid to Apple. You ask Apple for the refund.
