How to cancel Bumble

Our sending service does not work with Bumble because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Check how you paid: with Apple, with Google Play or in the Bumble app.

  2. 2

    Cancel in that same place, with the steps below.

  3. 3

    If you cannot get into Bumble, ask their support team for help.

What you need to know

Apple
Phone «Ajustes» (Settings), your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), Bumble, «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription).
Google Play
Google Play app, your profile, «Pagos y suscripciones» (Payments and subscriptions), «Suscripciones», Bumble, «Cancelar suscripción».
Bumble app
If you paid with PayPal, card or Apple Pay: profile icon, Subscription details, Manage your subscription and cancel.
How much notice to give
Cancel before the next renewal. You keep the benefits until the end of what you paid for.
What you need
Getting into your Bumble account or the store where you paid.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.bumble.com

Keep in mind

  • Asking for a refund does not cancel the renewal. Cancel it separately.
  • If Apple or Google Play refund your money, you may lose access at once.
