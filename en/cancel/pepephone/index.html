How to cancel Pepephone

Our sending service does not work with Pepephone because it only handles cancellations by phone. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Call 1706 or write on their social media with your full name, DNI and service details.

  2. 2

    You get an email confirming the cancellation with instructions for the router and a tracking code.

  3. 3

    Pack the router in a box and take it to Correos (Spanish post office): shipping is free.

What you need to know

Phone
1706. The account holder has to ask.
Social media
Pepephone's Twitter or Facebook, with your full name, DNI (Spanish ID card) and service details.
How much notice to give
2 days according to their contract.
Minimum term
Fibre on its own has no minimum term.
Equipment
Router: 60 calendar days to return it for free at Correos. If not, 90 € (including IVA, the Spanish VAT) on the next bill.
Your proof
The cancellation confirmation email.
What you need
Being the account holder. Full name and DNI. Service details.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: pepephone.com

Keep in mind

  • Cancelling Netflix or the multiSIM included in your plan has its own process.
