How to cancel Gemini

Our sending service does not work with Gemini because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Go to gemini.google.com.

  2. 2

    At the bottom, click «Ajustes» (Settings) and then «Gestionar suscripción» (Manage subscription).

  3. 3

    In the Google One settings, select «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription) and confirm with «Cancelar» (Cancel).

What you need to know

Gemini website
gemini.google.com, at the bottom «Ajustes» (Settings), «Gestionar suscripción» (Manage subscription). This opens the Google One settings to cancel.
Google One settings
The Google AI plan is cancelled in Google One, with «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription).
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
You keep the plan until the billing cycle ends.
Minimum term
None.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The Google One confirmation message.
What you need
Your Google account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.google.com

Keep in mind

  • When the AI Pro plan ends you lose Gemini in Pro, the extra storage for Drive, Gmail and Photos, Gemini in Gmail and Docs, and the included YouTube Premium Lite.
  • If you paid through the App Store, you cancel it in your Apple subscriptions.
  • If you are using more than 15 GB when you cancel, Gmail stops sending and receiving emails.
