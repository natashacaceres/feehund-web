How to cancel NordVPN

Our sending service does not work with NordVPN because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to your Nord Account.

  2. 2

    Open the billing tab («Billing»).

  3. 3

    Click «Cancel» next to automatic renewal and then «Cancel auto-renewal».

  4. 4

    Check that automatic renewal shows as turned off («off»).

  5. 5

    You will get a confirmation email.

What you need to know

Nord Account (web)
«Billing» tab, «Cancel» next to automatic renewal, «Cancel auto-renewal».
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
Before the next billing date. The service continues until the period ends.
Minimum term
None, but plans are paid in advance.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The cancellation confirmation email.
What you need
Your Nord Account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.nordvpn.com

Keep in mind

  • Cancelling the renewal does not refund your money. You request the refund separately through the chat.
  • There is only a refund within 30 days of the first purchase. Renewals are not refunded, unless the law says otherwise.
  • If you bought it through the App Store, Google Play or Amazon, you cancel it in that store.
  • The same steps work for NordPass, NordLocker, dedicated IP and bundles.
