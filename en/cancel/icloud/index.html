How to cancel iCloud+

Our sending service does not work with iCloud+ because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    First, download or delete whatever does not fit in 5 GB.

  2. 2

    On your iPhone open «Ajustes» (Settings) and tap your name.

  3. 3

    Tap «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions) and then iCloud+ under «Activo» (Active).

  4. 4

    Tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).

What you need to know

iPhone or iPad (iOS 18.4 or later)
«Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), iCloud+ under «Activo» (Active), «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
iPhone with iOS 18 to 18.3
«Ajustes» (Settings), your name, iCloud, «Gestionar Plan» (Manage Plan), «Opciones de Reducción» (Downgrade Options) and choose the free 5 GB plan.
Windows PC
iCloud for Windows, «Gestionar» (Manage), «Cambiar Plan» (Change Plan), «Opciones de Reducción» (Downgrade Options) and choose the free 5 GB plan.
Without an Apple device
Apple explains in its help pages how to cancel iCloud+ online.
How much notice to give
The change takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
Minimum term
None. It renews automatically each period.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Check that the subscription no longer shows as active or that the free plan has the blue tick.
What you need
Your Apple Account and its password.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.apple.com

Keep in mind

  • If you use more storage than you have left, iCloud stops syncing and making backups.
  • You lose «Ocultar mi correo» (Hide My Email), «Relay Privado» (Private Relay) and the other iCloud+ features.
  • If you changed plan recently, you have 14 days to ask Apple for a refund.
  • If you have Apple One, you can cancel iCloud+ and keep the Apple One storage.
