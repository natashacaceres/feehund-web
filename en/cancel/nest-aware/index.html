How to cancel Nest Aware

Our sending service does not work with Nest Aware because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    First save any clips and videos you want to keep. When you cancel you lose them.

  2. 2

    Check where you bought it. How you cancel depends on that.

  3. 3

    Google Store. Go to the subscription management page, sign in, choose the subscription and tap «Más > Cancelar suscripción» (More > Cancel subscription). Confirm with «Cancelar suscripción».

  4. 4

    Tienda Nest. In the Nest app tap «Configuración > Google Home Premium > Gestionar suscripción», choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción».

  5. 5

    If you bought it from a Google partner, ask them to cancel it.

What you need to know

Google Store
Google Store subscription management page, if you bought it there.
Google Play
If you bought it on Google Play, you cancel it on Google Play.
Nest app
If you bought it in the Tienda Nest (Nest Store). «Configuración > Google Home Premium > Gestionar suscripción» (Settings > Google Home Premium > Manage subscription).
Your proof
Before you confirm, a summary of the charges is shown.
What you need
Access to your Google or Nest account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.google.com

Keep in mind

  • Google Home Premium is the new name of Nest Aware.
