How to cancel Suno

Our sending service does not work with Suno because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    If you bought it on the web, go to suno.com/account.

  2. 2

    Choose «Cancel Subscription» at the top of the page and confirm.

  3. 3

    If you bought it on your phone, cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

  4. 4

    Do it before the renewal date.

What you need to know

Suno website
suno.com/account, «Cancel Subscription» at the top of the page. You can also cancel in Stripe with «Update Payment».
Help by email
billing@suno.com for help with billing; support@suno.com if only one or two days have passed since payment.
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
Before renewal. You keep using the service until the end of the cycle and then move to the Basic plan.
Minimum term
None.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Save a screenshot of the cancellation.
What you need
Your Suno account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: help.suno.com

Keep in mind

  • Payments are final and not refunded, unless Suno decides otherwise case by case.
  • They usually refund you if you paid by mistake or did not use anything. If only one or two days have passed, write to support@suno.com.
  • Suno usually cannot change App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
