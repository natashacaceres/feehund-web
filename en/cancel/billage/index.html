How to cancel Billage

Our sending service does not work with Billage because it is a service for businesses and sending for businesses will come later. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Write to bajas@getbillage.com saying that you want to close your Billage account.

  2. 2

    Include your name, your company's name and the account email address.

  3. 3

    Ask for a receipt and read confirmation.

  4. 4

    Do it at least 45 days in advance. The cancellation coincides with the end of a month.

What you need to know

Email
bajas@getbillage.com, in writing and asking for a receipt and read confirmation.
How much notice to give
45 days at least. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the month.
Minimum term
The contract lasts one year and renews automatically for the same period.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The receipt and read confirmation for your email.
What you need
Your Billage account details.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: getbillage.com

Keep in mind

  • If you give less than 45 days' notice, the cancellation is delayed.
  • If they change the price at renewal, they notify you within the 15 days before expiry.
