How to cancel Deezer

Our sending service does not work with Deezer because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to Deezer with your account.

  2. 2

    Go to «Ajustes» and to your subscription management.

  3. 3

    Tap «Cancelar mi suscripción», choose the reason and confirm. You will get a confirmation email.

What you need to know

Deezer app or website
«Perfil» (Profile) > «Ajustes» (Settings, gear icon) > manage your subscription > «Cancelar mi suscripción» (Cancel my subscription) > reason > confirm.
Apple, Google Play or partner
If you pay through Apple, Google Play or a partner (for example your provider), Deezer can't cancel it. Cancel with them.
How much notice to give
Cancel at least 48 hours before renewal. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period and until then you keep Premium.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. It renews automatically every month. Their terms say that, once the subscription is confirmed, they don't accept cancellations or refunds as far as the law allows.
Equipment
There is no equipment to return.
Your proof
The confirmation email from Deezer.
What you need
Log in to your Deezer account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.deezer.com

Keep in mind

  • If you cancel during a free trial, it stops the same day and you can't repeat the trial.
  • If you don't see the option to cancel, you may be on a Free account or have another account with a different email.
  • After cancelling, downloaded music no longer plays offline.
