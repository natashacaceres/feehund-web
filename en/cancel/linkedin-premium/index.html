How to cancel LinkedIn Premium

Our sending service does not work with LinkedIn Premium because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Click the «Yo» (Me) icon at the top of your LinkedIn home page.

  2. 2

    Choose «Funcionalidades Premium» (Premium features) and then «Gestionar suscripción» (Manage subscription), under «Detalles del plan» (Plan details).

  3. 3

    In the «Centro para administradores» (Admin Center), click «Compras» (Purchases) and choose your active subscription.

  4. 4

    Click «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription), under «Acciones» (Actions), and then «Confirmar cancelación» (Confirm cancellation).

  5. 5

    Do it at least one day before the billing date.

What you need to know

LinkedIn on a computer
«Yo» (Me) icon, «Funcionalidades Premium» (Premium features), «Gestionar suscripción» (Manage subscription), «Compras» (Purchases), your active subscription, «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription).
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
At least one day before the billing date. Premium features continue until the end of the cycle.
Minimum term
None.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Save a screenshot of the confirmation.
What you need
Your LinkedIn account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: linkedin.com

Keep in mind

  • If you paid through the App Store, you cancel it from your Apple device.
  • If you paid through Google Play, you can cancel it on LinkedIn or on Google Play.
  • If you bought it on your iPhone through the mobile website, you cancel it on the desktop version, not in the app.
  • If you did not cancel the trial in time, check LinkedIn's refund policy.
