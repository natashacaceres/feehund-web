How to cancel IONOS

Our sending service does not work with IONOS because it is a service for businesses and sending for businesses will come later. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to my.ionos.es with your account.

  2. 2

    Find the contract under «Contratos y suscripciones» (Contracts and subscriptions) and request cancellation of the contract or the service.

  3. 3

    You get a confirmation email with what is left to complete the cancellation. Follow it.

  4. 4

    If your offer has a minimum term and you want to leave early, fill in the early cancellation form and send it to facturacion@ionos.es with a copy of your NIF (Spanish tax ID).

What you need to know

Customer area
Cancellation tool on my.ionos.es. You can cancel the whole contract or just one service.
How much notice to give
Contracts with a fixed term or minimum period renew automatically unless you give notice 4 weeks before the end of the period.
Minimum term
If you have a minimum term and leave early, you pay the difference between your price and the price without a minimum term.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
The cancellation confirmation email and the final invoice.
What you need
Your customer ID and contract number. A copy of your NIF if it is an early cancellation.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: ionos.es

Keep in mind

  • Cancelling has no extra costs. If you paid in advance, they refund the corresponding part, except for domains and additional services.
  • If you cancel or transfer all your domains without cancelling the contract, the contract stays active and keeps being charged.
  • Each Premium service is cancelled separately.
