How to cancel Microsoft 365

Our sending service does not work with Microsoft 365 because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Go to account.microsoft.com/services/microsoft365 with the account you bought it with.

  2. 2

    Select «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). It may say «Actualizar» (Update) or «Cancelar» (Cancel).

  3. 3

    Review the page and at the bottom choose «No quiero mi suscripción» (I don't want my subscription).

  4. 4

    If you only see «Activar facturación periódica» (Turn on recurring billing), it is already set to end and you will not be charged again.

What you need to know

Your Microsoft account website
account.microsoft.com/services/microsoft365, with the account you used to buy the subscription, and «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription).
App Store, Google Play or retailer
If you bought it from the App Store, Google Play or a retailer such as Amazon, you cancel it with them.
iPhone (App Store)
If you pay through Apple: «Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android (Google Play)
If you pay through Google Play: open the Google Play subscriptions page, choose the subscription and tap «Cancelar suscripción» (Cancel subscription). Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
How much notice to give
You can use it until it expires.
Minimum term
None.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Check that the page shows «Activar facturación periódica» (Turn on recurring billing), a sign that it no longer renews.
What you need
Your Microsoft account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.microsoft.com

Keep in mind

  • You get a refund if you cancel within 30 days of purchase or before the first recurring charge. Also for the last recurring charge if you cancel within 30 days of payment, once per account and product.
  • When you cancel you go back to 5 GB in OneDrive and 15 GB in Outlook.com. If you are over, you cannot upload files or send or receive email.
  • After 6 months over the storage limit they may delete your OneDrive.
  • On Family or Premium, the storage of the people you share it with also goes down.
