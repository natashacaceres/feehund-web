How to cancel Reale

  1. 1

    Check the expiry date on your policy.

  2. 2

    Write that you want to cancel the policy at the expiry date. Include your name, NIF and policy number, and sign it.

  3. 3

    Send it with a copy of your NIF to incidencias.ret@reale.es at least one month before the expiry date.

  4. 4

    Ask for confirmation and keep it.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

Email
incidencias.ret@reale.es, with the signed letter and a copy of your NIF (Spanish tax ID).
How much notice to give
At least 1 month before the expiry date.
Minimum term
The policy renews automatically every year on the expiry date.
Your proof
The email you sent and Reale's confirmation.
What you need
Letter signed by the policyholder. Copy of the NIF. Policy number.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: reale.es

Keep in mind

  • The reale@reale.es address in the online purchase conditions is for withdrawal, not for cancelling at the expiry date.
