How to cancel Apple One

Our sending service does not work with Apple One because it does not accept cancellations sent by someone else. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    On your iPhone open «Ajustes» (Settings) and tap your name.

  2. 2

    Tap «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions) and then Apple One.

  3. 3

    Tap «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription). You may have to scroll down to see it.

  4. 4

    If there is no «Cancelar» button or you see an expiry notice in red, it is already cancelled.

What you need to know

iPhone or iPad
«Ajustes» (Settings), tap your name, «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), Apple One, «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Windows PC
In iTunes, your account, «Ver mi Cuenta» (View My Account), «Ajustes» (Settings), «Gestionar» (Manage) next to «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions), «Editar» (Edit) and «Cancelar Suscripción» (Cancel Subscription).
Android or web
For a subscription billed by Apple, go to account.apple.com with your Apple Account and follow the instructions.
How much notice to give
Cancel before renewal.
Minimum term
None. It renews automatically, also when the free trial ends.
Equipment
There is nothing to return.
Your proof
Check that the expiry notice appears in red.
What you need
The Apple Account you pay for Apple One with.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.apple.com

Keep in mind

  • If the receipt shows another family member's account, they have to cancel it. You cannot cancel a family member's subscription.
  • If you cannot find an Apple receipt, you may be paying another company and you have to cancel it with them.
  • If you want a refund, you request it at reportaproblem.apple.com.
