How to cancel Filmin

Our sending service does not work with Filmin because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Log in to filmin.es from the browser and go to «Configuración».

  2. 2

    Open «Suscripciones y tickets».

  3. 3

    Tap «Gestionar Suscripción» and then «Cancelar renovación».

What you need to know

Filmin website
filmin.es in the browser, «Configuración», «Suscripciones y tickets», «Gestionar Suscripción», «Cancelar renovación» (Settings, Subscriptions and tickets, Manage subscription, Cancel renewal).
iPhone or iPad
If you paid through Apple, «Ajustes», «ID de Apple» (Settings, Apple ID), and there you see your subscriptions and cancel them.
Android
If you paid through Google Play, cancel in Google Play «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions).
Amazon
If you paid through Amazon, cancel in the Amazon «Suscripciones» section.
Orange, Vodafone or SomConnexió
If it is part of your Orange or Vodafone tariff, cancel with them. With SomConnexió, tell the provider and also cancel in Filmin.
How much notice to give
Cancel before renewal. You can keep watching Filmin until the date your current payment ends.
Minimum term
Standard plans renew automatically and no minimum term is stated. The SomConnexió plus Filmin subscription has a 1 year minimum term. If you leave earlier, they charge you what is left, up to 72 €.
Equipment
There is no equipment to return.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of the subscription screen with the renewal cancelled.
What you need
Log in to your Filmin account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: ayuda.filmin.es
