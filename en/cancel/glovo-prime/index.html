How to cancel Glovo Prime

Our sending service does not work with Glovo Prime because it can only be cancelled from your account or from the store where you pay. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    Open the Glovo app and go to your profile, in «Mi cuenta».

  2. 2

    Go to Glovo Prime and select «darse de baja» (unsubscribe).

  3. 3

    Do it at least 2 working days before renewal so you are not charged for the next month.

What you need to know

App
Your profile, «Mi cuenta» (My account) section, where your Glovo Prime subscription is.
How much notice to give
At least 2 working days before the renewal date.
Minimum term
There is no commitment. It is monthly and renews automatically. After cancelling you keep using Prime until the day before the same date the following month. What you paid and partly used periods are not refunded.
Your proof
A screenshot of the cancellation.
What you need
Access to your Glovo account.

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: glovoapp.com

Keep in mind

  • Cancelling Prime does not delete your Glovo account.
