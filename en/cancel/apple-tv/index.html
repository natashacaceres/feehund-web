How to cancel Apple TV+

Our sending service does not work with Apple TV+ because it does not accept cancellations sent by someone else. So here are the steps to do it yourself.

  1. 1

    On your iPhone open «Ajustes» (Settings) and tap your name.

  2. 2

    Tap «Suscripciones» (Subscriptions) and choose Apple TV.

  3. 3

    Tap «Cancelar suscripción». If you see an expiry notice in red, it is already cancelled.

What you need to know

iPhone or iPad
«Ajustes > tu nombre > Suscripciones > Apple TV > Cancelar suscripción» (Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Apple TV > Cancel subscription).
Web
tv.apple.com/es > sign in > your account > «Ajustes > Suscripciones > Gestionar > Cancelar suscripción» (Settings > Subscriptions > Manage > Cancel subscription).
Mac or PC
On a Mac, «App Store > tu nombre > Suscripciones». On Windows, Apple TV app > «tu nombre > Ver mi cuenta > Suscripciones > Gestionar» (your name > View my account > Subscriptions > Manage).
Android
If you pay through Google Play, cancel in the Apple TV app on Android or in Google Play.
Amazon or provider
If you pay for Apple TV through Amazon, it can only be cancelled in Amazon. If it comes with your provider, cancel with them.
How much notice to give
It renews automatically until you cancel it. If you are on a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
Minimum term
There is no minimum term. You have 14 days from the receipt to withdraw and ask for a refund in «Cuenta de Apple > Historial de compras > Desistir de la transacción» (Apple Account > Purchase history > Withdraw from the transaction). After that period you can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com and Apple decides.
Equipment
There is no equipment to return.
Your proof
Take a screenshot of «Suscripciones» with the expiry notice in red. Apple sends a confirmation email.
What you need
Your Apple Account («Cuenta de Apple»).

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: support.apple.com

Keep in mind

  • If the subscription is on the account of another «En familia» (Family Sharing) member, only that person can cancel it.
  • If you can't find it, search your email for «recibo de Apple» (Apple receipt) to see which Apple Account was used.
  • If you have Apple One, Apple TV is included and is cancelled with Apple One.
