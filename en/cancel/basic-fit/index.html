How to cancel Basic-Fit

  1. 1

    Check in My Basic-Fit when your minimum term ends and when the next direct debit is.

  2. 2

    Cancel in the app, on the website or at the club kiosk.

  3. 3

    Or send an email asking to cancel with your name, membership number and the address of your home gym.

  4. 4

    Keep the confirmation email.

Would you rather we send it for you?

Letter ready and sent by certified mail. If it does not arrive, we refund you.

Send my cancellation · 4,99 €

What you need to know

My Basic-Fit app or website
From your account, free of charge.
Club kiosk
The orange self-service terminal at your gym.
Email
To customer service, servicioalcliente@basic-fit.es, with your name, membership number and the address of your «Gimnasio Principal» (home gym).
How much notice to give
Annual plan paid every 4 weeks: 1 cycle of 4 weeks, counted from the day after the next direct debit. Flex plan: 1 week.
Minimum term
The annual plan has a minimum of 52 weeks. To leave right when it ends, cancel up to 4 weeks before the end. If not, it continues indefinitely and then you can leave with 4 weeks' notice. The website advertises a joining fee of 19,99 €.
Your proof
The cancellation confirmation email.
What you need
Name. Membership number. Address of your «Gimnasio Principal» (home gym).

Checked: 11/11/2026 · Source: basic-fit.com

Keep in mind

  • You will keep paying during the notice period or until the end of the minimum term.
  • You can withdraw the cancellation in My Basic-Fit before the end date.
